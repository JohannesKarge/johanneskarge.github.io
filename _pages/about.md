Welcome! I'm Johannes Karge, a PhD candidate at **Paris School of Economics** and junior research economist at **Banque de France**.

## About My Research

My work sits at the intersection of **macroeconomics** and **financial stability**, with a particular focus on:

- Private sector debt dynamics
- Corporate finance and macroprudential policy  
- Climate risks in financial systems
- Economic history perspectives on financial crises

## Background

I bring international experience from roles at the **European Central Bank** and across multiple countries (Germany, Greece, Netherlands, Portugal, France). My research combines rigorous economic theory with data-driven empirical analysis using R, Python, and Stata.
